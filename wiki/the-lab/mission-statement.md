# Mission statement

Our goal is to make fundamental contributions to our understanding of the brain, of intelligence, and of what it means to be a thinking being; and to help develop methods that will enable progress on those topics going forward.

We aim to advance and develop the careers of scientists who join the lab, by improving their scientific thinking, training them on new skills, and supporting their creativity and productivity.

Finally, recognizing the positions of great luck and privilege many of us have been given, we aim to make science a more inclusive, more diverse, healthier, and happier community by promoting policies and practices that support these values, and continuing to educate ourselves on ways to achieve them.
